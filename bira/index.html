Battle Intelligence Response Arena

What it is

Configurable grid. Simultaneous turn resolution. You give a natural-language command, and the browser AI converts it into legal tactical actions.

How to play

Write an order in the command box, then click Execute turn. Your units and enemy units choose actions for the same turn. Attacks are resolved simultaneously, then surviving units move.

Core rules

Each unit occupies one cell. Units have HP, movement range, attack range, and damage. Actions are limited to MOVE and ATTACK. The AI can only choose legal action IDs generated by the game engine.

Simultaneous combat

If two units shoot each other in the same turn, both attacks happen before destroyed units are removed. This means both units can be destroyed in the same turn.

Victory and defeat

A side loses if it has no units or no legal moves. If both sides have no units or legal moves, the game is a draw. If the turn limit is reached, the side with more surviving units wins. Equal unit count means a draw. A turn limit of 0 means no limit.

Configuration

In Settings, you can change columns, rows, turn limit, unit count, HP, damage, movement range, and attack range. You can drag starting units on the setup board or edit their exact column and row. Columns continue as A, B, C... Z, AA, AB, AC...

Log colors

Green messages are good for the player. Red messages are bad for the player. Yellow messages mark the end of the game.
