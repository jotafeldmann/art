Flavia comes from the Latin *flavus*: golden, yellow, amber. So this piece is an amber light.

## Overview

A small game in a single `index.html`. A limited 3D board sits in the dark. On it walks Flavia: red curly hair, black dress, black flats, brown skin. Around her is a penumbra where you can still see the board, and inside it, an amber light that goes with her.

As she walks, music is made.

## Why this is code as art

### 1) Walking is composing
Every step plays a note. There is no score; the path is the melody and the pace is the rhythm.

### 2) Notes that always agree
The notes come from a procedural pattern, a pentatonic scale over a soft drone, so whatever path she takes, the music combines.

### 3) Light as memory
The path ahead lights up. The square behind her stays lit for a while. If she walks a lot, an amber trail stays on the board. When she stops, the board plays back what she walked, like something kept in amber.

## The process

1. Build a limited 3D board in the browser.
2. Shape the character from simple forms: curls, dress, flats.
3. Leave the board in penumbra and give her an amber light.
4. Map each square to a note, so movement becomes music.
5. Let the light stay behind her, and fade slowly.

## Objective

To give Flavia a piece she can play, where her walking becomes music and light.
